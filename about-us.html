Who we are

Ospina Talent Consulting was created by Yohanna Ospina, an executive leader from the technology sector with extensive experience across industries in digital transformation, innovation, consulting, technological implementation, infrastructure and operations. She founded the firm in January 2022 to help companies grow on the back of what technology can actually do for them.

The company is full of tech-loving people who simply want to make IT better. From the top down, we are all here to guide our partners and the industry a little further.
